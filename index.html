[01] About PACT

An Interdisciplinary
Student Collective

PACT is a student organization at the University of Calgary built around the intersection of artificial intelligence, emerging technology, public policy, law, privacy, digital rights, and civic impact.


We exist because AI is advancing faster than the policies, regulations, and public conversations surrounding it — and students across every discipline have a role to play in shaping what comes next. PACT is the space where that conversation begins.

[02] Why PACT

Why This
Matters Now

AI is reshaping law, democracy, healthcare, and public life in real time — but the policies and protections surrounding it are still catching up. Students need a space to understand these forces and help shape what comes next.

// 01 · URGENCY
Policy Can't Keep Up
Regulation and public understanding of AI lag far behind the technology. PACT helps students understand this gap — and how to help close it.
// 02 · GAP
An Unmet Space on Campus
No existing UCalgary club focuses specifically on AI governance, digital rights, and civic technology together. PACT fills that gap.
// 03 · DESIGN
Interdisciplinary by Design
The biggest AI policy challenges require law, engineering, ethics, public health, and political science in the same room. That's what PACT builds.
// 04 · IMPACT
Students Have a Voice
Tech governance is being shaped right now. PACT gives students the skills, network, and platform to contribute from day one.

[04] What We'll Do

Events &
Initiatives

PACT is a platform for building real skills, connecting with experts, and contributing to important conversations — not just listening to them.

// EVENT TYPE 01
Speaker Panels
Lawyers, policymakers, researchers, and tech professionals discussing AI, governance, and careers at the intersection of tech and policy.
// EVENT TYPE 02
Discussion Nights
Student-led conversations on emerging issues — from surveillance and data rights to AI regulation bills and digital democracy.
// EVENT TYPE 03
Workshops
Introductory sessions on policy analysis, digital rights, privacy tools, and more. No background needed — just curiosity.
// EVENT TYPE 04
Policy Brief Writing
Collaborative sessions to produce student-authored briefs on pressing AI and technology governance questions.
// EVENT TYPE 05
Reading Groups
Deep-dives into key texts, papers, reports, and legislation shaping the global AI policy landscape.
// EVENT TYPE 06
Club Collaborations
Joint events with pre-law, computer science, political science, cybersecurity, and health science clubs across campus.
[05] Who Should Join

Built for Every
Curious Student

PACT is designed for students from all programs and backgrounds. If you're curious about how AI and technology affect the world — you belong here.

Political ScienceLaw & Society Computer ScienceCybersecurity Data ScienceEngineering PhilosophyCommunications Health SciencesBusiness Public PolicyEthics Social WorkAnd More →

No background in technology or policy required. Whether you're technical, policy-focused, law-focused, or simply curious — PACT is for you.

Why join PACT? Because the future of AI governance, digital rights, and civic technology will be shaped by people who understand both the technology and its social impact.

PACT gives you the opportunity to build skills in research, writing, public speaking, event planning, policy analysis, and civic engagement — while meeting like-minded students across disciplines.

Explore career paths in law, public policy, AI governance, cybersecurity policy, responsible innovation, government, research, advocacy, and technology — all from one community.
